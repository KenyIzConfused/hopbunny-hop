How to install the brushes

I. Simple Way:

1. Put the brushes files (they end with .asset.blend) in a folder of your choice and name it something like 'Original Brush Pack'
2. Open Blender, and under the 'Edit' top-left menu choose 'Preferences'
3. Select the 'File Paths' section
4. Under 'Asset Libraries' click on the Plus sign button on the right to add a new Asset Library
5. Navigate inside the folder with the brushes and click the 'Add Asset Library' button
6. If you can see a 'Save Preferences' button in the bottom-left, click on it
7. Done.

II. Crazy Way:

A. If it's your first time creating or installing Brushes:

1. Choose any Grease Pencil brush
2. Go to the 'Tool' tab in the 'Properties' panel (on the right side usually)
3. On the right of the brush icon, click on the arrow button and choose 'Duplicate Asset'
4. You can now click on the arrow button and choose 'Delete Asset'
5. Open 'Preferences' under the 'Edit' top-left menu
6. Choose the 'File Paths' section, and under 'Asset Libraries' note the 'Path'
7. Navigate to the folder in the 'Path' from your documents browser
8. Inside that folder there should be a 'Saved' folder, enter it
9. Enter the 'Brushes' folder inside the 'Saved' folder
10. Paste the individual brush asset files there (they end with .asset.blend)

B. If you created or installed brushes before:

Start from step 5.
